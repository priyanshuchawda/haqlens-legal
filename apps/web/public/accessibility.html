Accessibility statement

HaqLens Legal is designed to help people understand legal information and prepare for professional support. It is not legal advice.

How the workspace supports access

  • Semantic headings, landmarks, labels, fieldsets, legends, and live status messages.
  • Keyboard navigation, a skip link, visible focus indicators, and focus management for confirmations.
  • Responsive layouts, readable contrast, Hindi and English interface copy, and safe error states.
  • Evidence remains linked to its source and stays in the browser unless an action explicitly sends it.

Feedback

If a control is difficult to use, please open an issue in the public repository with the page, browser, and keyboard or assistive technology details.
